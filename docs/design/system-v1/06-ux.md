# How Whim flows

Phase B of `design-system-v1`. Flow-level changes: fewer steps, clearer places, and the moments between
screens. Screen-level layouts are in `02-screens.md`; components in `03-components.md`; motion moments
(M-numbers) in `05-motion.md`. Evidence comes from the Android capture in
`Whim-evidence/design-audit-2026-10-09/android/` (file names below) and the production flowbench
(`openspec/changes/beta-1/flowbench/after-visible.json`, 22 runs on 2026-09-26).

## The map

Every surface is one of four kinds, and each kind has one way in and one way out.

| Kind | What lives there | Comes from | Leaves by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Root | Your apps | App launch | n/a |
| Full screen | A running app; Update required; Link missing | Its tile (morph), or the system | iOS edge swipe or Android back at the app's root; the orb's "Your apps" |
| Pushed screen | History, Settings, Advanced, AI features | A row or menu item | Back control, iOS edge swipe, Android predictive back |
| Sheet | The making flow, the Whim sheet, Report, confirmations, first-run consent | The thing you touched (composer, orb, tile, row) | Close button, drag down, scrim tap, Android back |

The context menu on a tile is the one anchored surface; it closes on any tap outside it and on back.

| Today | Becomes |
|---|---|
| 14 full screens swapped by `setScreen` with hard cuts | 4 full screens, 4 pushed screens, 5 sheet types, 1 menu |
| Compose, Clarify, Plan, Build, Done and Failure as separate screens with a 3-bar step indicator | One making sheet with four pages: Describe, Plan, Making, Ready (or Failure) |
| Age, Terms and Consent as three screens before the first prompt | One first-run sheet, shown only when making the first app |
| Orb menu with four rows, "Change it" leaving the app | Whim sheet with the change field at the top; the app stays open |

## 1. Making an app

### The steps

| Today (capture) | Taps | New | Taps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composer → Compose screen, type → Continue → Clarify, answer → Continue → Plan (`71`, `72`, which repeats the answers as rows) → Build it → Making → Open it | 5 + answers | Composer → Describe, type → Continue → Plan with the questions inline → Make it → Making → Open | 4, answers optional |

**Clarify and Plan merge into one page.** The capture shows why: clarify asks three questions
(`61-clarify-questions-top`), then the plan repeats those answers as three of its eight rows ("How much
should it hold? A few weeks", `72-plan-bottom`). One page puts the decisions next to what they decide:

- **Questions first, plan under them.** Clarify returns in about 1.8 s (p50) and the plan in about 2.3 s
  more. The page shows the questions as soon as they land, so the person is answering while the plan is still
  being written.
- **"I'll decide" is selected by default** on every question. Making an app without touching a chip is the
  normal path and now looks like it; today "Skip these and Whim will pick sensible answers" is a footnote
  under unselected pills.
- **The plan no longer repeats the answers.** The rewrite is told which decisions the questions leave open,
  and the answers travel to generation as `clarifications`, which `GenerateRequest` already carries. This is a
  server prompt change and a client sequencing change; the wire contract stays.
- **Rows still edit in place** (M25), and an edited row shows the person's words as typed.

The step bars go away. With two pages before making, the title ("What should it do?", "Here's the plan")
answers "where am I" on its own.

### While it's made

The making page has the wisp at the top, "Making Pour Timer", the four steps, and one honest line about
time. Time copy comes from the production flowbench: generation took 53 s at the median, 144 s at the 90th
percentile and 225 s at worst.

| Elapsed | Line |
|---|---|
| 0–75 s | "Usually about a minute. You can leave; it keeps going." |
| 75–150 s | "Taking longer than most. Still going." |
| Over 150 s | "This one's slow. I'll keep at it; you can leave." |
| No bytes for 40 s | "No word from the server for 40 s. Still waiting." with the stuck wisp |
| In line | "Waiting for a free spot. You're next." or "2 ahead of you." with the wisp asleep |

The thresholds live in one constant with the run that measured them, and are re-measured whenever the
flowbench runs before a model change. Later, the server can send live percentiles from its ledger; the copy
doesn't change shape when it does.

What goes: "Connected, waiting for a reply · 0:06" and "Writing · 3,560 characters"
(`81-build-progress-a`, `84-build-progress-d`). They describe the transport, not the making. The step list
says where the work is and the wisp says whether it's moving; elapsed time sits on the current step.

**Leaving is the default, and it's safe.** "Back to your apps" (or back, or a swipe down) collapses the sheet
into the ghost tile (M5), which keeps the ember glowing. Tapping that tile opens the sheet again. Stopping is
in the tile's menu ("Stop making") and on the page while it waits in line, as today.

### Ready

When the app arrives with the sheet open: the wisp flares, the app's tile rises out of it (M10), "Pour Timer
is ready", "It's on your home screen.", then **Open Pour Timer** in the app's tint and a plain **Done**.
"Report this app" moves out of this moment (`90-done` shows it under the buttons); it lives in the Whim sheet
and History, where the problem will actually be noticed.

When it arrives with the sheet closed: the ghost tile turns into the app's tile in place (M4) and the success
haptic plays. No toast; the tile is the news.

### When it doesn't work

The failure page (and a failed tile, which opens the same page) keeps one idea from today's screen: say what
happened, what's safe, and the one next step. It loses three stacked full-width buttons and the red title
(`131-failure-terminal-new-app`).

| Kind | Body | Primary | Plain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Couldn't get it to run, and rewording helps | "I tried 3 times and couldn't get it to run. Describing it differently often helps." | Change the description (`ink`) | Try again as it is |
| Couldn't get it to run, rewording won't help | "I tried 3 times and couldn't get it to run." | Try again (`ember`) | |
| The server refused for now | The server's reason, as a notice with a countdown | Try again (`ember`), disabled until the window ends | |
| Needs a newer Whim (`133-failure-fallback-newer-version` offers "Try again" today) | "Finishing this needs the latest Whim." | Update Whim (`ink`) | Not now |
| Stopped when the phone closed Whim | "Making stopped when Whim closed." | Try again (`ember`) | |

- When changing an app, a line with a check says "Your current version still works."
- Server reason strings ("Could not produce a buildable app after maximum attempts") map to these sentences
  by failure kind; unknown reasons show the server text.
- "What happened" (the run timeline) is a collapsed row at the bottom. The duplicated "What happened / WHAT
  HAPPENED" header in the details sheet (`88-build-details-sheet-empty`) goes away with it.
- "Discard" sits in the header's trailing edge as a text button in `danger-text`. Discarding happens at once
  and the toast offers Undo for 6 s before the attempt is deleted.

### When it can't be made as asked

The plan page shows "I can't make this as asked", the reason, and a card: "I could make this instead: a
packing list you fill in yourself". Primary **Make that instead** (`ember`), plain **Change my idea**. The
server's reason text says "Mini-apps can't fetch live weather" (`67-clarify-limit`); the prompt for the limit
reason gets the glossary ("apps", not "mini-apps").

## 2. Changing an app

Today "Change it" from the orb closes the app, opens the compose screen, and walks the whole flow; the person
next sees their app after reopening it. Changing is the core loop of the product, so it gets the shortest
path.

| From | Path |
|---|---|
| Inside the app | Orb → Whim sheet with "What should change?" already at the top → type → send → the sheet grows into the Plan page for the change → **Make the change** → the sheet collapses into the orb (M5 into the orb) |
| Home | Tile menu → **Change it** → the making sheet opens in change mode |
| History | The current version's **Change it** |

While a change is being made, the app stays open and usable on its current version. The orb's ember glows
with the stream (M20). Tapping the orb shows the change's status at the top of the Whim sheet, with Stop.

When the change lands: the orb flares once and a toast says "Pour Timer changed" with **Reload**. Reload
recreates the realm on the new version (the sanctioned reset, spike2 §5). Nothing reloads by itself, so
nobody loses what they were typing. Leaving and reopening the app also gets the new version.

When it fails: the orb shows a danger dot and the toast says "The change didn't work" with **See why**,
opening the failure page in a sheet.

| Taps to a change, from inside the app | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| | Orb, Change it, Continue, Continue, Make the change, Back to your apps, open the app again: 7, and the app closes | Orb, send, Make the change, Reload: 4, and the app stays open |

## 3. Using an app

- **Opening** grows the app out of its tile (M2). The tint fills the screen with the glyph until the app
  paints; "Opening…" appears only after 1.5 s. Today's boot overlay (`120-miniapp-booting`) goes.
- **Leaving:** iOS edge swipe or Android back at the app's root, or the orb's **Your apps**; all three shrink
  the app back into its tile (M3).
- **Back inside the app:** the SDK header's back control, the iOS edge swipe, Android back.
- **The orb** stays bottom-trailing (resolves `01-direction.md` §16.3). A top capsule, the usual answer in
  other mini-app hosts, would cover each app's header, take height from every app, and make apps feel like web
  pages in a wrapper; apps first argues for full-screen apps with a small, quiet control. The occlusion the
  capture shows (`121-miniapp-running-hello-app`, the orb over "Clear done") is handled two ways: the orb
  shrinks to a 44pt disc with a 52pt target, and `Screen` keeps padding the bottom by its footprint so the
  last element scrolls clear.
- **When an app crashes:** "This app ran into a problem", with **Reload** (`ink`) and a plain **Ask Whim to
  fix it**, which opens the change field prefilled with "Fix the error that stops this app." A crash becomes a
  change request instead of a dead end.

## 4. History

| Today | New |
|---|---|
| Two-colour title "Hello App history", a Report pill, filter pills ("All 1"), mono labels (`100-history-single-version`) | "History" with the app's tile and name under it; a "⋯" button holding Report a problem |
| Going back to a version behind a confirm sheet | Immediate, with a toast: "Back on version 3" and **Undo** |
| Making a copy behind a confirm sheet | The "same data or fresh?" question as a small sheet, then a toast: "Copy made" and **Open** |
| Six coloured kind badges, "↑ YOU'RE ON THIS ONE" | Neutral kind chips with icons; a `Current` badge |
| Filter pills by kind | Removed. Most apps have under 20 versions; a filter costs a row of chrome for a search nobody does |

Going back is safe to do without asking because every version stays in History and the newer one is one tap
away; Undo makes that visible. When going back would hide saved data, the expanded row says so before the tap:
"Some saved data won't show in this version. It isn't deleted and comes back when you return."

## 5. Your apps (home)

- **Title** "Your apps" in `largeTitle` and a settings button. The "Whim" title and the "YOUR APPS" eyebrow
  (`01-home-first-launch-examples`) said the same thing twice; the brand is in the composer's ember and the
  app icon (resolves §16.5).
- **Grid:** 4 columns of glyph tiles (`04-mini-app-icons.md`). Apps being made and failed attempts come first,
  newest first; then apps, newest first. An app doesn't move when it changes, so places stay learnable.
- **Search** appears under the title once there are 13 apps.
- **Composer** at the bottom, always reachable, morphing into the making sheet (M6).
- **Offline:** a neutral notice under the title: "Offline. Your apps still work; making new ones needs a
  connection." Today it is a 6pt grey dot.
- **Long-press** gives the context menu (M12): Open, Change it, History, Make a copy, Share link, then Delete.
  Today's sheet has eight rows including two red ones (Delete and Dismiss, `09-home-tile-menu-with-dismiss`).
- **Delete** asks in a confirm sheet: "Delete Pour Timer?", "It and everything saved in it will be removed
  from this phone. This can't be undone.", **Keep it** as the big button, **Delete** as red text. Today it's
  the platform's alert with uppercase CANCEL and DELETE (`07-delete-native-alert`).
- **Share link** opens the platform share sheet with the app link. Today a custom sheet shows the URL and
  tells you to press and hold it (`05-app-link-sheet`).
- **Copies** read "Copy" on their second line and take the next tint.
- **Empty** (every app deleted): the wisp listening, "Make your first app", and three idea chips that open the
  making sheet with the idea filled in.

## 6. First run

Today: home with three examples; the first tap on the composer walks Age (silent unless blocked), Terms, then
Consent, each a full screen (`10-terms-en`, `14`–`16-consent-ask`), before compose opens.

New: the first tap on the composer opens one sheet, "Before Whim makes apps for you":

1. A short summary in three rows with icons: what's sent, what stays on your phone, what we never do.
2. Links to the full privacy policy and terms, and a "Full details" row that expands the complete consent
   text in place.
3. A row "I accept the Terms of use" that must be ticked.
4. **Agree and continue** (`ink`), enabled once the terms row is ticked; **Not now** under it.
5. "Français" / "English" at the trailing edge of the header.

Two separate acts (accepting the terms, agreeing to the data use) on one sheet instead of two screens. They stay
two acts on purpose: consent to data use should be distinguishable from accepting terms (GDPR article 7(2)),
and both are still recorded with their versions as today. The legal text itself doesn't change here.

Age stays silent: the store's answer arrives within its 3 s deadline while the sheet is open, and only a
blocked answer shows its own screen.

The examples stay as normal apps. Their History says "Made by Whim as an example".

## 7. Settings

The owner's note sets the rule: common settings first, diagnostic controls one level down.

| Level | Rows |
|---|---|
| Settings | AI features (value "On", opens the review screen) · About: Privacy policy ↗, Terms of use ↗, Support ↗, Version · Advanced › |
| Advanced | Send error details (switch, with its explanation as the group footer) · This phone: Phone ID (with a copy button), Make a new ID (confirm sheet) · Server: Whim's server ✓ / Your own server (confirm sheet, then the address field and its check line) |

- **Highlighting** is removed (resolves §16.6). The simplified marks pass contrast and survive being rendered
  flat; the switch was an accessibility hedge and an experiment flag, and both jobs are done.
- No in-app Reduce Motion switch. The system setting is honoured everywhere, including inside apps; a second
  switch would be a setting to explain.
- Each row is a grouped-list row, not a separate card per row (`30-settings-top`).

## 8. Platform behaviour

| Surface | iOS | Android |
|---|---|---|
| Pushed screen | Back chevron; edge swipe pops interactively | Back arrow; predictive back previews the pop |
| Sheet | Drag down; close button; scrim tap | Same, plus back closes it |
| Running app | Edge swipe goes back in the app, then closes it into its tile | Back goes back in the app, then closes it into its tile |
| Making sheet | Drag down leaves it running | Back leaves it running |
| Context menu | Tap outside | Tap outside or back |
| Text in apps | Follows Dynamic Type | Follows the font size setting |
