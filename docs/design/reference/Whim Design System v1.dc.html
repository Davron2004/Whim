01 · Colour
Paper is the shell. Colour belongs to apps.

The launcher is warm neutral with one accent. Saturated colour is reserved for app tiles and status — so when something is coloured, it means something.

02 · Type
Two faces. Grotesk speaks, Mono measures.

Space Grotesk for everything the user reads as language. IBM Plex Mono only for things that are literally counted or timed — clocks, durations, version numbers, step counters, section eyebrows. Never mono for prose.

04 · Tile identity
Ghost letterform

A solid tile in the app's assigned colour, the monogram set small on the baseline at bottom-left, and the same monogram blown up to ~85% of the tile height bleeding off the top-right at 16% white. A 1 px inset white border at 30% keeps it from going flat. Placeholder until apps carry their own icons.

05 · Buttons & busy
Nothing is ever just there.

Every action that waits on the network has a busy state on the control that started it. The label changes to plain words — never a bare spinner, never a greyed-out button with no explanation. Buttons are 52–56 px tall, full width, at the bottom of the screen.
