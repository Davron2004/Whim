## Owner decisions that shape beta-1 (don't re-litigate)

- **The installed pre-beta builds are not a constraint** (2026-09-25). Break the wire. They're retired by
  the protocol header's 426 and then the minimum builds (design D16/D17).
- **Forward compatibility is layered:** tolerant reader, then protocol-level negotiation
  (`x-whim-protocol`), then an in-band `compat` fallback from the frozen set `skip | fail | update`, then
  forced update. It's declarative, never server-sent code. It must ship in beta-1 because the first
  tester build is the oldest reader forever (design D16).
- A real queue on the stream (`queued` events), the clarify `limit` arm, a mid-stream retry with `restart`,
  and clarify answer modes (one or many, "Other", "Decide for me"): all in beta-1.

## Standing instructions (carried over)

- Run attended console and account steps yourself (ego-browser where a CLI falls short). Don't ask permission.
- Never pay for anything or sign a binding agreement. Prepare it and leave the final click to the owner.

  Pre-launch production has no users, so deploys need no per-step ask.

  only after it passes on a **newly created** simulator and a fresh emulator.
