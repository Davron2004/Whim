Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

AnyCognition Inc. runs Whim. Reach us at {{WHIM_SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What leaves your phone

To build or change an app, Whim sends what you ask for to our server. AI companies that work for us write the code.

What gets sent

  • What you ask for: your description, your answers and the plan you approve
  • When you change an app: its name, code and description, and the layout of its data, never the data itself
  • An ID Whim makes for this phone, used for daily limits and usage totals.
  • Error details when something goes wrong. They’re technical only, not what you typed or saved.

Why

To build your apps and run Whim: daily limits, stopping abuse, keeping costs in check, and finding and fixing problems.

Who gets it

AnyCognition, the company that makes Whim, and companies that do work for us, like cloud hosting and AI providers. Some of them are outside Canada. They can’t train AI on it or use it for their own products, though some may keep it for a short time for security and legal reasons. Apple or Google may also check that requests come from the real Whim app. We give information to authorities when the law requires it.

What you save in your apps

Nobody at Whim can read it. It stays on your phone, and anything Whim ever syncs or backs up for you is encrypted on your phone with a key Whim never has.

What we never do

Show ads, sell your data or share it for advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

Requests go to AnyCognition’s server, which sends them on to third-party AI models through OpenRouter. Which models Whim uses can change without notice, and the providers behind them may process your request outside Canada.

If we ever want to collect a new kind of information, use it for a new purpose, keep it longer, or give it to a new kind of company, we’ll ask you first.

You can turn AI features and error details off in Settings. Apps you already have keep working either way.

Reports

Reports are sent only when you tap Send report. They go to AnyCognition, not to the AI model providers. A report includes the reason you picked, any note you wrote, the app's name, and your phone's anonymous ID. It also includes the reported version's original code when that code is saved on your phone, so we can investigate what went wrong. You can choose whether to include the prompt that made that version. Older apps may have no saved code or prompt to include. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Usage ledger

Whim also keeps a usage ledger: one row per request, holding your phone's anonymous ID, the request type, timestamps, the outcome, token counts and cost. The ledger holds no request content, and is kept for 90 days.

What Whim doesn't do

Whim has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. There is no way for people to share apps or content with each other.

Server logs hold no request content, and the front proxy that serves Whim keeps no access log.

Your choices

Turning AI features off in Settings stops requests to make or change apps. Apps already on your phone keep working. You can still choose to send a report.

For access or deletion requests, contact {{WHIM_SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Nothing here is tied to a name or account, so we may not be able to find one person's rows — and reports and ledger rows are gone after 90 days either way.

Age rating

Whim is rated 13+. If what Whim sends ever changes, the app asks you to agree again.
