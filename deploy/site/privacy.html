Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

AnyCognition Inc. runs Whim. Reach us at {{WHIM_SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What leaves your phone

To make or change an app, Whim sends your request to AnyCognition’s server. The server uses AI models from other companies, reached through OpenRouter, to write the app.

What gets sent

  • What you ask for: your description, your answers to Whim’s questions, and the plan you approve
  • When you change an app: its code, its current description, and the layout of its saved data
  • An anonymous ID for this phone, used for daily limits

What never gets sent

Anything you save inside your apps

Requests go to AnyCognition’s server, which sends them on to third-party AI models through OpenRouter. Whim is currently configured to use {{WHIM_ENGINEER_MODEL}} to write and change apps, and {{WHIM_REWRITE_MODEL}} to rewrite them.

You can turn this off in Settings. Apps you already have keep working either way.

Reports

Reports are sent only when you tap Send. A report holds the reason you picked, an optional note, and — if you chose to include them — the app's name, prompt and source, stored together with your phone's anonymous ID. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Usage ledger

Whim also keeps a usage ledger: one row per request, holding your phone's anonymous ID, the request type, timestamps, the outcome, token counts and cost. The ledger holds no request content, and is kept for 90 days.

What Whim doesn't do

Whim has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. There is no way for people to share apps or content with each other.

Server logs hold no request content, and the front proxy that serves Whim keeps no access log.

Your choices

Turning AI features off in Settings stops all sending. Apps already on your phone keep working.

For access or deletion requests, contact {{WHIM_SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Nothing here is tied to a name or account, so we may not be able to find one person's rows — and reports and ledger rows are gone after 90 days either way.

Age rating

Whim is rated 13+. If what Whim sends ever changes, the app asks you to agree again.
