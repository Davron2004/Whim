# Handoff 236: iOS native switch keeps Apple's haptic (PO ruling 35)

Commit 4ea3a022 on chain/design-system-v1-236 (BASE df7d770f).

## Behaviour
| Path | iOS | Android |
|---|---|---|
| Flip of the native switch itself (`Switch.onValueChange`) | no Whim haptic (UIKit plays its own) | `toggle-on`/`toggle-off` |
| Row press (Pressable around the row) | `toggle-on`/`toggle-off` | `toggle-on`/`toggle-off` |

Basis: a row press changes the native UISwitch only through its `value` prop (setOn:animated:), which
UIKit does not buzz for; only touch on the switch does. So the row press keeps ours on iOS and every flip
gives exactly one haptic. Android's Switch has no built-in haptic. (From UIKit behaviour, not measured on a
device; owner may confirm on an iPhone.)
