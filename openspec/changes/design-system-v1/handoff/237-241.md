## #237: Home is an empty row in the iOS back button's long-press menu: DONE
- Cause: Home's stack entry had no title, and `headerFor` gave the root `{ hidden: true }` with no
  title. react-native-screens 4.28 sets `navitem.title = config.title` even when the header is hidden
  (`RNSScreenStackHeaderConfig.mm:508-513`). UIKit's back menu reads that title.
- Fix: `stackTitle` returns `COPY.homeTitle` ("Your apps") for Home, and `headerFor` gives the root
  `{ hidden: true, title }`. Home's header stays hidden and its safe-area edges are unchanged
  (`ownHeader` still treats the root as drawing its own header).
- Visible side effect (expected iOS behaviour, please confirm on device): at the default text size,
  Settings' back button now reads "‹ Your apps" instead of "‹ Back", because `configureBackItem` uses
  the previous item's title. At large text it is still the chevron alone (#218).
- Files: `src/host/launcher/NativeStack.tsx`, `src/host/launcher/LauncherRoot.tsx`.
- Test: `native-stack-ui.suite.tsx` "pushed screens carry the platform header…" asserts that Home's
  header is hidden and carries `COPY.homeTitle`, and that Home still draws its own title.

## #241.2: the toast's words overlap the list while it fades: DONE
- Cause: one `opacity` on the whole capsule. Mid-fade, the capsule is translucent and its words are
  half drawn, so they sit on top of the list text behind it (a-make2.png).
- Fix (`src/host/ui/Toast.tsx`): the same `fade` value now drives two opacities. The capsule's is
  `min(1, 2·fade)`. The words' (a new `Animated.View` row wrapping the message and the action) is
  `max(0, 2·fade − 1)`. On the way out, the words fade while the capsule is still opaque, and the
  empty capsule fades after. On the way in, the order is reversed. Durations, the 16 px rise (M14),
  swipe and Reduce Motion are unchanged. The words row copies the capsule's row/gap/flexShrink, so
  the layout does not change (TopHighlight is absolute).
- Test: `shell-surfaces-ui.suite.tsx` "Toast: on every frame of its fade in and out…" holds the
  animations, steps them 8 ms at a time through enter and leave, and asserts on every frame that
  capsule opacity < 1 ⇒ the words' own opacity is 0. It also checks that both opacities pass through
  values strictly between 0 and 1.

## #241.1: closing the Failure page from the Making sheet lands on Home: BLOCKED (class B)
- The real mechanism (reproduced with a temporary probe test in the real LauncherRoot, then removed)
  is not where the brief's map pointed. The iOS pass sent the change from the Whim sheet BEFORE
  consent had been granted (i-c1.png is the first-run sheet). `onWhimSend`'s legal branch
  (`LauncherRoot.tsx` ~:3440) never sets `under`. The first-run sheet is not `overApp`, so the app is
  torn down under it (probe: `at ask: app=false`). After Agree, the change continues as a change made
  from Home: Describe, then plan, then **Making page** (`making=true`), then the attached Failure page
  with no app → `closeSheet` → `goHome`. With consent already granted (the Android pass), the run
  starts detached and both of its paths already return to the app (pinned by existing tests).
- A Making page over a running app does not exist by construction ("Making never does: a change made
  from the running app collapses into the orb"). So "fix at close / keep `under` through making"
  has no app to return to.
- The fix needs a decision on the consent flow (ai-data-consent delta: "open the first-run sheet over
  the current screen… after agreeing the action SHALL continue as if consent had already existed";
  "Declining SHALL return… to Home when that screen was a running mini-app"). Options:
  A. Keep the app running under the first-run sheet when it comes from the Whim sheet's send (set
     `under` in the legal branch, and let `appUnder` accept terms/consent-ask/age-checking screens
     whose `returnTo` is that launch). Agreeing then continues exactly as with consent: plan over the
     app, collapse into the orb, failure via the toast or See why, close back to the app. Decline still
     goes Home per the spec, though the app was visibly running under the sheet. The owner should
     confirm that, or amend the spec to "back to the app". This needs the comments at
     `consent-flow.ts:93-99` updated.
  B. Keep today's behaviour (the first-run ask closes the app). Add to system.md §9 that a change
     whose first-run ask closed the app continues as one made from Home.
- Not done: the system.md §9 sentence ("closing that Failure page returns to the app"). It would only
  be true after option A.

## #241.3: the See why row is gone on reopen after a stray tap into History: CAUSE FOUND, not fixed
- `changeOver` (`LauncherRoot.tsx:343`) scopes the failed change to the launch:
  `rec.updatedAt >= on.tappedAt`. History opened from the app and left with Back (or a swipe) reopens
  the app through `onOpen`, which gives it a NEW `tappedAt`. The failure now predates the launch, so
  the See why row and the orb's danger dot both vanish. That matches the device note.
- Not fixed, because the lifetime is a design question: system.md §9 says the row shows "while a
  change of the app has failed and none is being made" (no launch scope), but the code deliberately
  scopes it per launch. Options: (a) carry the launch's change window across a History round trip
  (e.g. `HistoryScreenState` keeps the launch's `tappedAt`, and `onOpen` takes a `since`). (b) Drop the
  launch scope and show any undiscarded failed change of the app. That would also show it on a fresh
  open from Home, and the Home tile already says "Change didn't work".
