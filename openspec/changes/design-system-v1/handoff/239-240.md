## Why iOS differs (measured, CoreText on this Mac, system font 12 pt medium)
- Cell text width = ⌊(402 − 40)/4⌋ − 2×4 = **82 pt** on iPhone 17; 85 on a 412 dp Pixel, 94 on 448 dp.
- "Needs update": 80.8 pt bare, **82.5 pt** with the caption token's +0.012 em (0.144 pt/glyph) → over 82.
  Android Roboto ≈ 75.5 dp (from the a4 screenshot). "Change didn't work": 115.5 pt iOS, ≈110 dp Android:
  wider than any phone column, so it was cut on Android too.
- Root cause: SF opens its own spacing at small sizes (optical size: "Needs update" per pt = 6.73 at
  12 pt vs 6.43 at 17, 6.21 at 24), Roboto does not; the token tracking is added on top on iOS.
  Dropping positive tracking on iOS alone would fit "Needs update" on a 402 pt iPhone (80.8 ≤ 82) but not
  on 390/393 pt ones, and never "Change didn't work"; it is a global §2.7 type change, so not done here
  (option for the PO: iOS caption/footnote/callout tracking → 0; filed as #242, `ai-proposed`).
