## Findings
1. MEDIUM (verification.md side finding, confirmed; interaction defect). Tapping a Home tile within ~1.5 s of tapping "Back to your apps" is dropped: Home stays idle for 3 s+. 4 of 5 tries (`qa-2..5.png`, `quick-tap.mov`, contact sheet `seq-all.png`: Home idle for ~8 frames at 2 fps); try 1 opened. Taps >= 2 s after Back opened 12 of 12 (loop `trial`, with 0.4 s extra gap ~2 s total). The morph back into the tile lasts ~0.4 s, so the dead window is much longer than the animation. Expected: a tile responds as soon as Home is visible (system.md section 4 morph; no input shield after it ends). Driver latency per tap ~0.7 s, so the real window is somewhere between 0.75 and 2 s; a finger test would pin it.
2. LOW. Opening a tile soon after launch is slow: tapToPaint 1.8-1.9 s with `webView: 'loading'` (taps 0.5 s and 1.5 s after launch), 518 ms at 4 s. A forced `'cold'` was not produced. Whether 1.9 s is acceptable is not specified (system.md 4.7 says "measured with and without the warm WebView; may not regress it"); record the number.
3. INFO. The iOS edge swipe leaves only nav-stack pushes (Settings, Advanced, AI features, Report, History). Full-height sheets/screens (compose, Plan, Ready, Update-required, Failure, Link-missing) ignore it, the running app at depth 0 ignores it, and Link-missing also ignores swipe-down, so its only exit is the button. launcher-screen-exits only requires a visible control, so no violation; but a user swiping back from Link-missing sees nothing happen.
4. INFO (same as a3 finding 4). Report opened from the Whim sheet: Back and Cancel return to the Whim sheet, Close X to the app; content-reporting says the app is still running either way. Edge swipe started over the scrim (y=400) closes the Whim sheet, copy sheet and tile menu; that is a scrim drag, not a back gesture.
5. INFO. Describe focus (i1/i2 finding 1): opened from the Home composer with the keyboard up on every open I made after consent (before any make, and after Close), including after a Close. One open without the keyboard (chips showing) followed an edge swipe that left the compose sheet open; not a clean repro of i2's "after any make" case, no make was in flight. Left as i2 recorded it.
